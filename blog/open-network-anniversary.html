Pi Open Network:
One Year In!

February 20, 2026

Happy first anniversary of Open Network, Pioneers! Open Network, which launched on February 20, 2025, was a pivotal point in Pi’s history, connecting Pi’s blockchain, identity-verified community, and Web3 ecosystem with the external world. 

Prior to Open Network, the Pi community collectively built out the ecosystem over six years to ensure Pi’s readiness and sustainable utility. This developmental period allowed Pi to create real apps and utilities for Pioneers to engage with, and verify the identities of millions of Pioneers to prepare the network for real world assets and production processes. 

From the start, Pi was designed to be freely accessible, allowing anyone to mine without technical or financial barriers. That design not only permitted wide distribution and inclusivity, but it also enabled the network and all participants to afford the patience to engage in the difficult work necessary to establish a fully functional ecosystem predicated on utility.

Since then, the network continues to make progress in line with Pi’s long term vision and strategy—to create an inclusive, utility-driven, and widely adopted cryptocurrency that is broadly accessible. Progress in the first year of Open Network includes advances in Pi’s ecosystem activity, app development initiatives and tools, and innovative product and platform-level utility releases. A few highlights include the Pi App Studio, Pi Network Ventures, DEX/AMM/token creation features, and more. Ecosystem Token Design on Mainnet

Pi ecosystem tokens are community-created tokens issued on Pi that are designed to support real utility and product innovation. Pi emphasizes that the network design for ecosystem tokens—how ecosystem tokens are integrated into the Pi ecosystem and used to support real products and services—is the most critical factor when enabling ecosystem token creation on Mainnet.

Across the broader crypto space, a core issue is the misalignment between token issuance and real innovation. Despite tokens being a powerful feature in Web3, most tokens on other crypto networks function primarily as tools to raise capital—but such projects frequently fail to provide meaningful utility and useful products. This misalignment between token issuance and product creation is a structural problem in Web3. 

Pi is uniquely positioned to provide a different path because of the collective resources the network has, e.g. a large community with real users for real products. Pi’s approach is to integrate crypto tokens directly into the evolutionary process for products and innovations. To this end, Pi’s current launch programs encourage a new design construct based on Pi’s existing resources and infrastructure: utility tokens designed for user acquisition. This means projects issue tokens to fulfill the need to acquire users for their products (not to raise capital) and integrate these tokens for use cases inside their products. Users hold projects accountable for the products they provide, provide feedback for product iterations for improvement, and have meritocratic and transparent access to acquire the utility token issued by the project. Pi is staked and used in these mechanisms to orchestrate and facilitate the process. 

Watch the video (or read the video script) where the Pi Founders provided an overview on the design thinking behind ecosystem tokens on Pi. 

Key differentiating features of this new construct include:

  • All proceeds committed in Pi to acquire the tokens by participants are not transferred to the project that issues the token, unlike the typical token issuance model. Rather, all proceeds will be irreversibly deposited into the Liquidity Pool, which supports the stability and health of a token. 
  • Projects issuing tokens through this new launch construct need to have a working product in place, in the form of a functional app, as a prerequisite to issue a token through this program. 
  • The engagement with the project’s product by the participant may give certain favorable terms or benefits to the participant during a launch. 

Review Ecosystem Token Design Proposal in Detail and Leave Feedback

Pi is releasing specific details on the Ecosystem Token Design on Pi Network as a Pi Request for Comment (PiRC) to the community. Given the importance of the ecosystem token layer of the Pi ecosystem and the innovative approach and design, such design should be evaluated with input from the community and iterated in practice to improve. Keep in mind the design intentionally differs from existing models to address issues explained previously, and the design is meant to align tokens as a powerful feature of Web3 with the creation of the real-world products and innovations, drawing on the existing strength and resources of Pi Network.

Pioneers can read the full details of the ecosystem token design on GitHub. Feedback is welcome, and Pioneers are encouraged to comment, discuss with other community members and share specific suggestions on specific sections of the design using GitHub features like issues or pull requests. For those who do not have a GitHub account, a Google Form will also be available to submit high-level feedback. 

Pi will review and consider community input from GitHub and high-level feedback submitted through the Google Form. As with any design process, not all suggestions will be adopted, but feedback will be evaluated to determine whether and what adjustments are appropriate. The construct design is also subject to further revisions based on feedback from the projects that want to participate in the launch program.

Expanding the Utility of Pi KYC

Pi KYC is foundational to the integrity and authenticity of the network. More broadly, Pi views identity verification as a critical unsolved challenge in Web3 and a necessary step before mass real-world productions move on-chain.

But beyond Pi Network’s internal use, the next stage of this initiative is treating KYC itself as a service—KYC as a platform capability. Identity is fundamental to any meaningful integration with the real-world economy. Any transfers of value and ownership require knowing who is transferring and who is receiving. This is a fundamental identity—and therefore KYC—problem.

In the near term, Pi will offer the technology and product of Pi KYC solution (without sharing any user data) as a service to Web3 and traditional businesses. Pi’s distinct approach to KYC offers third-parties unique benefits:

  • Global geographic coverage
  • Scalability
  • Hybrid model combining AI and human validation workflows
  • Sanction checks for compliance
  • Cross-network comparisons
  • Support for different regulatory formats

Looking longer term, Pi’s identity-verified network itself, being a powerful network-level collective resource, is designed to enable services and use cases to build and integrate on top. Users retain control over their own identity and data, but they also gain the option to participate in applications built on top of an identity-verified network. That network, in turn, provides utility to external services that need identity-verified human participation.

This matters well beyond Pi. Solving KYC is a prerequisite for many major challenges in Web3, including real-world assets. More broadly, in an AI-driven world filled with bots and agents that can increasingly mimic human behavior, the ability to answer a simple but critical question—who is real—becomes essential.
