Pi2Day 2025 生态系统挑战赛

2025-06-27

The Ecosystem Challenge begins today and runs through July 7, 2025.

Pi2Day is here! As we celebrate another year of growth and progress, we’re inviting Pioneers to participate in this year’s Pi2Day Ecosystem Challenge—an interactive, self-guided journey through the Pi ecosystem with fun rewards along the way.

This challenge offers Pioneers the chance to explore features like Pi App Studio and Ecosystem Directory Staking, in addition to other parts of the ecosystem. With Open Network, this is the time for users to engage more deeply than ever with the apps, tools, and community that make Pi unique.

Whether you’re an app builder, active community member, or just curious to explore, this challenge is for you!

As you complete actions in the Ecosystem Challenge, you’ll unlock a series of exclusive in-app rewards that mark your achievement and showcase your contributions to the Pi ecosystem.

How to Get Started

  1. Open your Pi mining app and tap the Ecosystem Challenge button on your home screen. 
  2. Follow the list of actions. Each one will prompt you to explore, engage, or complete a meaningful step within the Pi ecosystem. 
  3. Complete all the steps to unlock special rewards and celebrate Pi2Day with the global Pioneer community!

Happy Pi2Day!
